How to Earn Points

Points are earned by logging fitness activities at Indianapolis parks. Different activities earn different points per minute:

Walking: 1 point/min
Running: 2 points/min
Cycling: 2 points/min
Hiking: 3 points/min
Swimming: 3 points/min

Leaderboard Rules

  • Leaderboard rankings are updated in real-time as activities are logged
  • Only activities performed at Indianapolis parks count toward points
  • The leaderboard resets at the beginning of each month
  • Monthly winners may receive special badges on their profiles
